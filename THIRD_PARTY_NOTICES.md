## `bdpdx/ATtiny10Programmer`

This companion tool is designed to communicate with the Arduino sketch from:

<https://github.com/bdpdx/ATtiny10Programmer>

The upstream repository describes the sketch as an ATtiny10 programmer and
publishes it under the MIT License. The upstream repository also contains its
own historical author credits and attribution notices; consult its
[`README.md`](https://github.com/bdpdx/ATtiny10Programmer) and

C++ frontend uses the sketch's public serial command interface.
